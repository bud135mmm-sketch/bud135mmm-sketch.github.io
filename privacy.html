Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Toolbench ("we," "this site") provides free calculators, converters, and utilities. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What this site does not do

Every tool on Toolbench — the unit converter, percentage calculator, BMI calculator, loan/EMI calculator, compound interest calculator, discount calculator, age calculator, GPA calculator, word counter, and password generator — runs entirely in your own browser. Numbers and text you type into any of these tools are never sent to a server, never stored, and never seen by us. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad preferences, at google.com/settings/ads and in Google's own Privacy & Terms page.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies. Any cookies present on this site come from the advertising script above, controlled by Google, not by us.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally as the site changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the last update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the site's GitHub repository.
