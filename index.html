About Toolbench

Toolbench is a small set of everyday calculators, converters, and utilities, each built to answer one specific question well rather than trying to be an all-in-one dashboard. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent to a server, logged, or stored, which also means every result appears instantly with no network delay.

The tools were picked because they're searched for constantly but rarely found in a fast, clean, distraction-free form: working out a loan payment before signing anything, checking how compound interest actually grows a savings account, converting a recipe's measurements, or generating a password that isn't "password123." Each tool has its own page with a short guide explaining the concept behind it, not just the calculator itself.

Feedback on any tool, or requests for new ones, are always welcome. See the privacy policy for details on what information this site collects (in short: none).
