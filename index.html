About Toolbench

Toolbench is a small collection of everyday calculators and unit converters, built to answer one quick question at a time — no sign-up, no app to install, nothing saved about you. Every calculation on this page runs directly in your browser: none of the numbers you type are sent anywhere or stored on a server. More tools get added over time based on what's genuinely useful day to day, from quick unit conversions to basic financial estimates like loan payments.

Have a tool you wish existed here, or spot something that gave you a wrong answer? Feedback is always welcome — this is a work in progress, built to get more useful over time.
