Unit converter

Converts between the length and weight units people run into most often — kilometers and miles, kilograms and pounds, and Celsius and Fahrenheit for temperature. Useful when reading a recipe from another country, checking a road sign's distance, comparing a shipping weight limit, or just double checking a number someone gave you in units you don't normally use. Pick a conversion from the list, type in the value, and the result updates instantly — nothing is rounded behind the scenes beyond standard two-decimal display.

Percentage calculator

Answers the two percentage questions that come up constantly: what a given percent of a number actually is (useful for tips, discounts, taxes, or commission), and what percentage one number represents of another (useful for tracking progress toward a goal, a test score, or a budget split). Enter both numbers once and get both answers at the same time, rather than running two separate calculations.

BMI calculator

Body mass index is a simple ratio of weight to height, commonly used as a rough screening tool rather than a diagnosis. Enter your height in centimeters and weight in kilograms to see your BMI and which general range it falls in — underweight, normal, overweight, or obese, using the standard World Health Organization thresholds. BMI doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, age, or sex, so it's a starting point for a conversation with a doctor, not a substitute for one — particularly for athletes, older adults, or anyone with an atypical body composition.

Loan / EMI calculator

Estimates the fixed monthly payment (EMI, equated monthly installment) for a loan with a set interest rate and term — the same math banks use for mortgages, car loans, and personal loans. Enter the amount you're borrowing, the annual interest rate, and the repayment period in months to see your monthly payment, the total interest you'd pay over the life of the loan, and the total amount repaid. It's a useful way to compare offers from different lenders or see how shortening the term changes the total interest cost — though your actual bank statement may differ slightly depending on how they compound interest or apply fees.

Age calculator

Works out someone's exact current age in years, months, and days from a birth date — more precise than just subtracting years, since it correctly accounts for whether this year's birthday has already passed and how many days are in the months in between. Handy for form fields that want an exact age, checking eligibility cutoffs, or just settling an argument about how old someone actually is today.

About Toolbench

Toolbench is a small collection of everyday calculators and unit converters, built to answer one quick question at a time — no sign-up, no app to install, nothing saved about you. Every calculation on this page runs directly in your browser: none of the numbers you type are sent anywhere or stored on a server, and none of the tools require an internet connection once the page has loaded. That also means results are instantaneous — there's no round trip to a server waiting on a network request.

The tools here were chosen because they're the kind of thing people search for constantly but rarely find in a clean, ad-free, fast-loading form: converting a measurement from a recipe or a road sign, checking a discount or a tip, working out a rough BMI, comparing loan offers, or figuring out someone's exact age. More tools get added over time based on what's genuinely useful day to day, and existing ones get refined as feedback comes in.
